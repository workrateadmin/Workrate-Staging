Add a Proposal Acceptance and Deposit system to WorkRate.

IMPORTANT:
Do not remove, redesign or break the existing quoting, invoicing, enquiry, job management or scheduling functionality.

The tradesperson must ALWAYS approve the final price before anything is shown to the customer.

CORE WORKFLOW:

1. Customer submits an enquiry.
2. WorkRate AI may create a draft quote.
3. Draft quote is INTERNAL ONLY.
4. Tradesperson reviews and edits all prices.
5. Tradesperson clicks "Approve & Send".
6. Only then does the customer receive the official proposal.
7. Customer can:
   - Accept Proposal
   - Ask a Question
   - Decline Proposal
8. If accepted, WorkRate requests the configured deposit.
9. Once deposit is confirmed as paid, update the proposal/job status to "Deposit Paid".
10. The remaining balance must automatically be calculated and tracked.

IMPORTANT SAFETY RULE:

AI-generated prices must NEVER automatically become customer-facing prices.

Only a price explicitly approved by the authenticated business owner can be sent to a customer or used to calculate a deposit.

- price
