Add an Automatic Enquiry Confirmation system to WorkRate.

IMPORTANT:
Do not change or break the existing website widget, enquiry creation, quoting, proposal, deposit, job, scheduling or invoice systems.

GOAL:

Immediately after a customer successfully submits an enquiry through the WorkRate website widget, automatically send them confirmation that their enquiry has been received.

The enquiry MUST be successfully saved to the database before any confirmation is sent.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE:

After submitting an enquiry, the customer should see:

"Thanks, [First Name] — your enquiry has been sent to [Business Name]. They'll be in touch soon."

Then trigger the business's configured customer confirmation methods.

EMAIL CONFIRMATION:

Subject:
"We've received your enquiry – [Business Name]"

Example message:

"Hi [First Name],

Thanks for getting in touch with [Business Name].

We've received your enquiry regarding [Project Type / Enquiry Title].

Your details have been passed through and we'll be in touch as soon as possible.

Thanks,
[Business Name]"

Include:
- Business logo where available
- Business name
- Phone number
- Email
- Website

SMS CONFIRMATION:

If SMS is enabled and the customer supplied a valid mobile number, prepare this message:

"Hi [First Name], thanks for contacting [Business Name]. We've received your enquiry about [Project Type] and will be in touch soon."

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT attempt to send a real SMS yet if no SMS provider has been configured.

Build the SMS confirmation system so a provider such as Twilio can be connected later without redesigning the enquiry workflow.

If SMS is unavailable:
- Do not fail the enquiry
- Do not show the customer an error
- Continue with email confirmation where available
- Record that SMS was not sent because no provider is configured

WORKRATE BRAIN SETTINGS:

Add:

WorkRate Brain → Customer Communications

Allow each business to configure:

Enquiry Confirmation:
[ON/OFF]

Email:
[ON/OFF]

SMS:
[ON/OFF]

Allow the business to edit its confirmation message.

Provide a sensible WorkRate default message.

Include a "Preview Customer Message" option.

IMPORTANT:
Do not allow businesses to accidentally remove essential information such as their business identity.

LOGGING:

Record against the enquiry:

- confirmation_email_status
- confirmation_email_sent_at
- confirmation_sms_status
- confirmation_sms_sent_at

Possible statuses:

pending
sent
failed
not_configured
disabled

The enquiry itself must NEVER fail because a confirmation email or SMS fails.

FUTURE-PROOFING:

Build one reusable Customer Communications service.

It should later support:

- Enquiry received
- Proposal ready
- Proposal accepted
- Deposit received
- Appointment confirmation
- Appointment reminder
- Job scheduled
- Invoice sent
- Payment reminder
- Payment received

Do NOT build all of those automations now.

Only build ENQUIRY RECEIVED at this stage.

TEST:

Submit a website enquiry containing:
- Name
- Email
- Mobile number
- Project information

Confirm:

1. Enquiry is saved correctly.
2. Customer sees successful submission message.
3. Email confirmation is queued/sent when email is configured.
4. SMS is handled gracefully when no SMS provider exists.
5. Enquiry appears normally in the WorkRate dashboard.
6. No duplicate confirmations are sent if the page refreshes.
7. Failure of email/SMS does not lose or duplicate the enquiry.

After implementation, explain what was added and what remains to be connected for live email and SMS delivery.
