Add automated customer email sending when a proposal is approved.

IMPORTANT:
Do not remove or redesign the existing Proposal Acceptance & Deposit system.

Current working flow:
Draft quote
→ Tradesperson clicks "Approve & Send Proposal"
→ Proposal token/link is created
→ Customer can view /proposal/:token
→ Customer can Accept / Ask a Question / Decline
→ Deposit workflow continues from there

NEW REQUIREMENT:

When the tradesperson clicks "Approve & Send Proposal", WorkRate must automatically send the customer a professional branded email containing the proposal link.

Do NOT require the tradesperson to copy and paste the proposal link manually.

EMAIL CONTENT:

Subject:
"Your proposal from [Business Name] is ready"

Email body should include:

- Business logo
- Business name
- Customer first name
- Project title
- Short project summary
- Total proposal value
- Deposit required
- Proposal expiry date if configured
- A large button:
  "View Proposal"

The button must link to the customer's unique public proposal URL:
 /proposal/:token

Below the button include:

"If you have any questions, you can reply to this email or use the Ask a Question option inside your proposal."

Add business contact details in the footer.

BRANDING:

Use the business's:
- Logo
- Brand colours
- Business name
- Email
- Phone
- Website

from WorkRate Brain / Documents & Branding.

EMAIL STATUS TRACKING:

Add proposal email fields to the database if they do not already exist:

- email_sent_at
- email_delivery_status
- email_error
- email_recipient

Proposal statuses should support:

- Approved
- Sent
- Viewed
- Accepted
- Deposit Awaiting Payment
- Deposit Paid
- Declined
- Expired

When email sending succeeds:
- Set proposal status to "Sent"
- Record email_sent_at

If email sending fails:
- Do NOT lose the proposal
- Keep the proposal link available
- Show a clear error to the tradesperson
- Add a "Retry Email" button

Add a "Resend Proposal" button after the first email has been sent.

PRE-SEND VALIDATION:

Before sending, confirm:
- Customer email exists
- Proposal has been approved
- Proposal token exists
- Business sender details exist

If customer email is missing, do not send.
Show:
"Customer email address required before proposal can be sent."

SECURITY:

Never expose private business dashboard URLs in the email.
Only use the public proposal token URL.

Do not include internal notes, AI reasoning, cost margins, or private pricing information.

TEST FLOW:

Use a test proposal and verify:

1. Tradesperson approves proposal
2. Customer receives email
3. Email contains correct business branding
4. "View Proposal" opens correct public proposal
5. Proposal status changes to Sent
6. Opening proposal changes status to Viewed
7. Customer can Accept / Question / Decline
8. Existing deposit workflow continues without breaking

Do not mark complete until the full email flow has been tested successfully.

After implementation, explain:
- Which email provider/service is being used
- Which database fields were added or changed
- How failed emails are handled
- How resend works
- How proposal status changes are tracked
