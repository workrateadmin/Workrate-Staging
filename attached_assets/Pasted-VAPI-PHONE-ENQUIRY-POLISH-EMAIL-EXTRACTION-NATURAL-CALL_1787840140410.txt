VAPI PHONE ENQUIRY POLISH — EMAIL EXTRACTION + NATURAL CALL ENDING

GOAL:
Make two small improvements to ALL future Vapi phone enquiries:

1. Reliably extract customer email addresses from transcripts
2. Make the AI receptionist end calls naturally and politely

Do NOT redesign the phone flow.
Do NOT change Zadarma routing.
Do NOT create a new assistant.
Do NOT hardcode Huntley Bespoke.
Do NOT publish automatically.

==================================================
1. EMAIL EXTRACTION
==================================================

CURRENT ISSUE:
The customer’s email is often present in the Vapi call transcript, but WorkRate does not reliably extract and save it into the enquiry/customer email field.

REAL EXAMPLE:
Transcript:
"Is jaman, J-M-A-N, dot hunsley. H-U-N-T-L-E-Y, at gmail.COM"

Expected normalized email:
jman.huntley@gmail.com

IMPLEMENT reusable transcript-email parsing for ALL future phone enquiries.

Rules:

- "at" → @
- "dot" → .
- "underscore" → _
- "dash" / "hyphen" → -
- "plus" → +

IMPORTANT:
If a speech-recognized word is immediately followed by explicit letter-by-letter spelling, prefer the spelled letters.

Examples:
"jaman, J-M-A-N" → jman
"hunsley, H-U-N-T-L-E-Y" → huntley

Explicit spelling should override a likely speech-to-text error.

Support:
- literal emails
- spoken emails
- spelled emails
- underscores
- hyphens
- plus addressing
- customer corrections

Validate the final candidate before saving.

If one valid email is confidently identified:
- save it to the existing enquiry/customer email field
- preserve transcript as source evidence

If conflicting candidates exist:
- do not guess
- flag "Email needs confirmation"

If no valid email is found:
- leave email blank
- keep the phone enquiry
- flag "Email needs confirmation"

Do not create duplicate enquiries/customers.

==================================================
2. NATURAL CALL ENDING
==================================================

CURRENT ISSUE:
The AI receptionist sometimes ends the call too abruptly with wording like:
"Bye"

GOAL:
End every completed call in a warm, professional way.

Preferred behaviour:

After confirming the enquiry is captured, the assistant should say something natural such as:

"Perfect, I've got all of that. Someone from {{businessName | default: "the business"}} will be in touch. Have a good day."

or:

"Thanks for calling {{businessName | default: "us"}}. We'll be in touch soon. Have a good day."

Then end the call.

RULES:

- Do not abruptly say only "bye"
- Do not end while the customer is still speaking
- Do not repeat the farewell
- Do not overdo it or sound robotic
- One short natural closing sentence is enough
- If the customer says goodbye first, respond briefly and politely before ending
- Use the actual business name dynamically where available
- Keep the existing multi-tenant assistant design
