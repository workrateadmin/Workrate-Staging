WORKRATE — ADD AI RECEPTIONIST USAGE TOP-UPS

GOAL:
Allow customers to purchase additional AI Receptionist call minutes when they approach or reach their included monthly allowance.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT remove or weaken the existing hard-cap usage protection.
Do NOT allow unlimited provider spend.
Reuse the existing billing, Stripe, entitlement, usage ledger, subscription-period, and allowance systems.

==================================================
1. TOP-UP PRODUCTS
==================================================

Add configurable AI Receptionist minute packs.

Initial pack sizes:

- +100 minutes
- +250 minutes
- +500 minutes

Do NOT invent final prices yet.

Each pack must support:
- minutes included
- customer price
- Stripe product/price mapping
- active/inactive
- sort order
- optional expiry policy

If no valid price is configured:
show "Price not set"
and do not allow purchase.

==================================================

==================================================
3. WARNING STATES
==================================================

At 75% usage:
show a soft warning and Buy More Minutes action.

At 90%:
show stronger warning.

At 100%:
AI Receptionist remains hard-capped for new calls,
but clearly show:

"You've reached your included call allowance."

[Buy more minutes]

Do not terminate calls already in progress.

==================================================
4. ALLOWANCE CALCULATION
==================================================

Effective monthly allowance must equal:

base plan allowance
+
active add-on allowance
+
purchased top-up minutes for the current billing period

Example:

Complete base allowance:
300 minutes

Customer buys:
+250 minutes

Effective allowance:
550 minutes

Usage enforcement must use the effective allowance server-side.

==================================================
5. BILLING PERIOD
==================================================

Top-ups should attach to the current Stripe billing period.

Default launch policy:
top-up minutes expire at the end of the current billing period.

Do not roll unused minutes forward unless explicitly enabled later.

Clearly tell the customer before purchase:

"Extra minutes are valid until your current billing period ends on [date]."

==================================================
6. STRIPE
==================================================

Use one-time Stripe purchases for minute packs.

Do not modify the recurring subscription amount.

Flow:

customer selects pack
→ Stripe Checkout
→ successful payment
→ verified webhook
→ top-up entitlement added
→ effective allowance increases

Do not grant minutes from the browser success redirect alone.

==================================================
7. WEBHOOK / IDEMPOTENCY
==================================================

Top-up purchases must be webhook-authoritative.

Prevent duplicate allocation from:
- webhook retries
- repeated redirects
- replayed events

Use Stripe checkout/payment ID as the dedupe source.

One payment must grant minutes exactly once.

==================================================
8. SERVER-SIDE ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

All AI Receptionist allowance checks must include purchased top-ups.

Frontend display is informational only.

A user must not be able to spoof:
- pack size
- price
- granted minutes
- billing period
- purchase completion

==================================================
9. ADMIN CONFIGURATION
==================================================

Extend the protected pricing admin area so WorkRate admin can configure:

- +100 minute pack price
- +250 minute pack price
- +500 minute pack price
- pack availability
- expiry policy

Do not expose admin pricing controls to trade customers.

==================================================
10. USAGE DISPLAY
==================================================

Show a clear breakdown if top-ups exist.

Used: 412 mins
Remaining: 138 mins

==================================================
11. ENTITLEMENT RULES
==================================================

Only businesses entitled to AI Receptionist may buy AI Receptionist top-ups.

Examples:

Core without AI Receptionist add-on:
cannot buy minute packs

Core + AI Receptionist:
can buy packs

Complete:
can buy packs

==================================================
12. CANCELLATION / PLAN CHANGE
==================================================

If the customer cancels or changes plan:

- existing purchased top-up minutes remain valid until the current billing period ends
- do not refund automatically
- do not carry top-ups into a future subscription period unless explicitly supported later

==================================================
13. INTERNAL COST TRACKING
==================================================

Top-up revenue should appear in the internal profitability foundation.

Track:

- recurring subscription revenue
- top-up purchase revenue
- verified provider costs
- gross contribution

Do not expose provider cost to customers.
