WORKRATE — ONBOARDING + PLANS + PAID TRIAL + BILLING FOUNDATION

GOAL:
Build a polished new-customer onboarding and subscription structure for WorkRate.

The customer journey should be:

Sign up
→ guided onboarding
→ choose plan
→ start 7-day paid trial
→ connect integrations
→ test WorkRate
→ use app
→ automatically move onto normal monthly billing after trial unless cancelled

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the whole app.
Do NOT rebuild quotes, jobs, invoices, finance, integrations, Vapi, HMRC or the existing WorkRate core.
Reuse existing business settings, branding, integrations, permissions and account data.

==================================================
1. PRICING MODEL
==================================================

WorkRate should support:

CORE PLAN
Target normal price:
£29/month

COMPLETE PLAN
Target normal price:
£99/month

ADD-ONS
Architecture must support optional paid add-ons such as:

- AI Receptionist
- Social AI / Meta integrations
- AI Concept Visuals
- Advanced Finance / MTD
- Cost Intelligence
- future integrations/features

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT hardcode final add-on pricing yet unless already confirmed in configuration.

Use a configurable plans/add-ons model so prices and allowances can be changed without rebuilding the app.

==================================================
2. COMMERCIAL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Pricing should be structured so:

Core is affordable.

Customers can add only the services they need.

Buying several add-ons should naturally make the £99 Complete plan better value.

Complete means:
ALL feature categories unlocked

but NOT unlimited provider usage.

AI, voice, messaging, image generation and other variable-cost services must still have usage allowances.

Do not advertise "unlimited" unless genuinely unlimited.

==================================================
3. 7-DAY PAID TRIAL
==================================================

Do NOT create a completely free trial.

WorkRate should support a 7-day paid introductory trial.

Preferred initial structure:

Core trial:
£5 for 7 days

Complete trial:
£20 for 7 days

If add-on trial pricing is required, make it configurable rather than hardcoded.

After the 7-day trial:

- automatically begin normal monthly billing for the selected plan
- unless the customer cancels before renewal
- show the renewal date and future price clearly before payment

Do not use misleading "50% off monthly price for 7 days" wording.

Customer-facing wording should be simple, for example:

"Try WorkRate Complete for 7 days for £20.
Then £99/month.
Cancel before [date] and you won't be charged the monthly subscription."

==================================================
4. PAYMENT PROVIDER
==================================================

Before implementation, inspect whether WorkRate already has Stripe or another billing provider configured.

If Stripe already exists:
reuse it.

If no subscription provider exists:
build this using Stripe Billing / Checkout unless there is a strong technical reason not to.

Do NOT collect raw card numbers inside WorkRate.

Use provider-hosted or secure embedded checkout.

Do not expose secret API keys to the browser.

==================================================

New businesses should have a guided first-run onboarding.

Suggested order:

1. Welcome
2. Business Details

10. Ready

Step 4 of 10

- Back

- Skip optional setup
- Finish later

11. ADD-ONS
==================================================

Design add-ons as separate configurable products.

Social AI
AI Concept Visuals
Advanced Finance / MTD

==================================================
12. FEATURE ENTITLEMENTS
==================================================

Create a clean entitlement system.

The app must be able to answer:

- which plan is this business on?
- which add-ons are active?
- which features are available?
- what usage allowance remains?
- is the account in trial?
- is billing active?
- is payment overdue/cancelled?

Do not scatter plan checks across random components.

Use a central server-side entitlement model.

Frontend may display entitlement state but must not be the source of truth.

==================================================
13. USAGE METERING FOUNDATION
==================================================

Build a usage-metering foundation for variable-cost features.

Track categories such as:

- AI receptionist minutes
- AI chat/messages
- concept image generations
- other provider usage where appropriate

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent final usage allowances yet.

Allowances should be configurable per plan/add-on.

The customer should later be able to see usage such as:

AI Receptionist
82 / 200 minutes

Concept Visuals
6 / 10 used

Do not allow client-side manipulation of usage.

==================================================
14. OVERAGE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Do NOT silently generate unlimited provider costs.

The billing architecture should support:

A. stop/pause usage at limit
B. optional overage charging
C. usage packs
D. plan upgrade

For initial launch, choose the safest simple behavior unless an overage system already exists.

Prefer:
warn customer before limit
then block/pause expensive usage at limit
rather than generating unbounded costs.

==================================================
15. TRIAL EXPIRY
==================================================

- days remaining
- trial end date
- normal renewal price
- current selected plan

Send/display reminders where existing notification/email infrastructure supports it.

Suggested reminders:
- 3 days remaining
- 1 day remaining
- renewal/converted to paid

Do not send duplicate reminders.

==================================================
16. CANCELLATION
==================================================

Customer must be able to:

- view subscription
- view renewal date
- cancel subscription
- manage billing method

Cancellation should not delete their WorkRate business data.

At cancellation:
- retain data
- restrict paid functionality according to the chosen product policy
- clearly show account state

Do not destroy customer records automatically.

==================================================

- payment pending

Trial
Payment required

Cancelled

22. SECURITY
==================================================

Confirm:

- subscription belongs to correct tenant/business
- billing webhooks are signature verified
- provider customer/subscription IDs are server-controlled
- feature entitlement is enforced server-side
- users cannot change plan by editing frontend requests
- secrets never reach browser
- no cross-tenant subscription access
- idempotent webhook handling
- duplicate webhook delivery does not duplicate billing state

Do not trust browser redirects alone to establish paid status.
