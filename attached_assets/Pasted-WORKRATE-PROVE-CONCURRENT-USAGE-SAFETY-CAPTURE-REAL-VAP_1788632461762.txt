==================================================

1 message

Fire:

- exactly 1 reservation succeeds
- exactly 1 provider action is allowed
- remaining 4 are rejected before provider spend

- no negative allowance
- no overspend

B. CONCEPT VISUALS

1 generation

Fire:

- no more provider calls start than allowed
